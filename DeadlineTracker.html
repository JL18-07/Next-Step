How to use NextStep

  1. Select Add application and enter the school, scholarship, exam, or program.
  2. Enter the official deadline and application link. The app calculates the days remaining.
  3. Update the status as you prepare and submit your application.
  4. Use the requirements checklist to track documents you still need to collect.
  5. Check this app regularly. It saves information in this browser on this device.

Privacy note: Your entries are stored locally in this browser. They are not sent to a school or uploaded to an online account. Export a backup if you change devices or clear browser data.

Reminder: NextStep is an organizer, not an official admissions source. Always verify dates, eligibility, and requirements with the institution.
